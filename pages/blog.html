Baking & Ordering

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to custom cake ordering and event catering questions. Still not sure? Just drop us a message!

Do you offer eggless or sugar-free options?

Yes! We offer a full range of eggless baked goods and traditional sweets, and can prepare sugar-free options using natural sweeteners like stevia.

What is the typical lead time for custom cakes?

Custom cakes require a minimum lead time of 3-5 days. For larger celebrations like weddings, we recommend booking 2-4 weeks in advance.

Do you collaborate directly with event planners?

Absolutely. We routinely partner with event planners, corporate coordinators, and caterers. We provide custom dessert layout previews and match hampers to your theme.

Do you deliver custom tiered cakes to venues in Chennai?

Yes. We provide temperature-controlled delivery and venue setup for multi-tiered cakes across Chennai, ensuring they arrive fresh and pristine.

What sweet packaging customization options do you offer?

We offer premium boxes (12, 24, 36 pieces), custom logo labels, satin ribbons in your brand colors, and personalized cards for corporate and festive gifts.
