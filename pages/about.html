Our Mission

Heritage, Synergy

Founded in 2012 by Kavya Sundaram, Sweet Symphony began as a vision to bring custom-made celebratory cakes and traditional Indian sweets to the local community in Chennai. What started as a boutique kitchen has grown into a premier bakehouse and sweet shop, home to master confectioners, traditional sweet makers, and expert pastry chefs.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Artisanal Excellence

Every ingredient is measured down to the gram, ensuring the perfect texture, rise, and sweetness in every single treat.

Quality Sourcing

We source pure cow ghee, Kashmiri saffron, organic cardamoms, and premium dry fruits for unparalleled richness.

Certified Hygiene

Our kitchens strictly follow FSSAI safety codes, keeping prep areas spotless and sanitizing daily.

Tailored Celebrations

We design customized wedding sweet boxes, corporate hampers, and themed cakes tailored to your preferences.
