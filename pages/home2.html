Culinary Rigor

Quality Sourcing & Hygiene Standards

We bake beyond cosmetics. Every sweet and loaf undergoes intensive taste checks, hygiene controls, and fresh ingredient curation.

Ingredient Sourcing & Purity

  • Ghee & Dairy Purity: We test milk and ghee purity to ensure absolute richness and no synthetic additives.
  • Premium Saffron & Spice: We source grade-A Kashmiri saffron and organic cardamom pods for robust flavor.
  • Premium Dry Fruits: Hand-picked cashews, almonds, and pistachios checked for size and freshness.

Moisture & Temperature Control

  • Proving Cabinets: Proving temperatures are tuned to maintain optimal humidity levels for light, airy croissants.
  • Deck Oven Moisture: High steam levels are injected during the initial bake to lock in moisture and produce a glossy crust.
  • Sweet Cooling Timelines: Sweets are cooled in temperature-controlled rooms to achieve the perfect firm bite.

Food Safety & Hygiene Codes

  • FSSAI Compliance: We prep all items in sterile, stainless steel kitchens matching international food safety standards.
  • Allergen Labeling: All boxes clearly list ingredients, nutritional details, and allergen warnings (gluten, dairy, nuts).
  • Zero-Preservative Rule: We use organic sugar syrups and natural cooling methods to ensure a healthy shelf life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer eggless or sugar-free options?

Yes! We offer a full range of eggless baked goods and traditional sweets, and can prepare sugar-free options using natural sweeteners like stevia.

Can you match custom branding for corporate events?

Yes! For bulk sweet orders (50+ boxes), we offer custom-designed packaging with your company logo, matching ribbons, and customized greeting inserts.

What food safety standards do you follow?

All our kitchens are FSSAI certified. We maintain strict hygiene protocols, dust-free proving spaces, and daily sanitation of baking equipment.

Do you offer delivery and venue setup for weddings?

Yes. We provide temperature-controlled delivery and have a dedicated team to set up multi-tiered cakes and beautiful sweet spreads at your venue.
